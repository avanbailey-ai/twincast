FAQ

Questions, answered.

What is a digital twin?

A rights-controlled digital version of you, created from a guided 60-second phone scan plus an ID and liveness check. Brands can license it for AI and e-commerce imagery, but only for uses you approve.

What’s the difference between booking and licensing?

Book means a brand hires you in person, with a standard contract, escrowed pay and shoot-day check-ins. License means a brand requests a scoped use of your digital twin (channel, region, term, product). You approve or decline it and earn royalties per use.

Can a brand use my twin without asking?

No. Every use needs your approval and is scoped by channel, region, term and category. Outputs are watermarked and each asset gets a rights certificate tied to a consent record.

What if I change my mind?

You can revoke future use in one tap. Brands sign contracts that require them to remove content, and there’s a fast reporting flow if something looks wrong.

Who can join as a model?

Anyone 18 or older can join. Commercial twin licensing requires 18+ ID and liveness verification, and no minors are permitted for commercial licensing. We especially want diverse, size-inclusive models (00–4X+), since fit modeling is about measurements, not follower counts.

What happens to my scan data?

Twin data is encrypted. It is never sold, and never used to train third-party models without your opt-in.

Which brands is Twincast for?

We’re starting with Shopify and DTC clothing brands that need on-model imagery across sizes for PDPs, ads and lookbooks. Beauty, lifestyle/UGC and agencies come later.

Is Twincast live?

Not yet. Twincast is pre-launch, and the first version will be a mobile website. Join the waitlist and we’ll contact you as early access opens.
